# SAT Reading and Writing Vocabulary

**Less common words from every question in the bank, with short definitions**

These 825 words come from the passages, questions, and all four answer choices (correct and incorrect) of the 1,843 practice questions in `CB_SAT_English_QuestionBank`, at every difficulty level. Each one is rare in everyday English: 86% occur fewer than about three times per million words, and nearly all fewer than ten. Names, technical science terms that the passages define (such as species and chemicals), and words whose meaning is obvious from a common root (*carelessly*, *unimportant*) are left out. The Hard-question list in the [study guide](SAT_Reading_and_Writing_Study_Guide.md) (section 4.1) is included here.

- **195** words appear in at least one correct answer and **401** in at least one wrong answer (292 of them only in wrong answers). **249** were answer choices in Words in Context questions, the question type that tests vocabulary directly.
- Definitions give the sense used in the bank first. Related forms that appear in the bank follow the headword in italics.
- **Question** gives one question that uses the word: ✓ means the word is in the correct answer, ✗ means it is in a wrong answer, and no mark means it is in the passage or the question. Search the folder for the 8-character ID (Ctrl+Shift+F) to see the word in context.
- *corpulent* does not appear in the bank; it was added by request and is marked "not in bank".
- Old-fashioned words from poems and plays (*thou*, *'tis*, *ere*) are in a separate table at the end.
